Wireframe — Head-to-Head "Compare Two" flow — Mobile (412px → shown at 300px)
The mobile compare flow for deciding what to play. First an intent screen ("what are you in the mood for?") scopes the pool; then the Beli pattern: two sparse cards side by side with an OR badge — tap the one you prefer and the next pairing loads. On phone it's a full-screen takeover. The winner stays; the unchosen card is replaced by the next game in line.
Annotations — mobile reflows of the compare flow
1Full-screen, not a modal. Phones have no room for a dimmed-backdrop modal, so compare takes the whole screen; a back chevron + Return to Library backs out. This matches the existing mobile "compare two" bar, which already promised a full-screen flow.
2Two cards side by side with an OR badge — the Beli layout. Tap the card you prefer; the winner stays and the unchosen game is replaced by the next in line, until one is left standing.
3Cards are deliberately sparse — cover, name, genre, time-to-finish. The dense trust signals (match · friends · streak) were removed: head-to-head is a half-second gut pick, and reading kills the speed the pattern depends on.
4Undo / Skip — minimal controls. Undo steps back a pairing; Skip passes on this matchup. No scores or stars.
5The outcome is full-screen too — the winning game with a one-line "why it won." The single confident answer pulled out of a stalled backlog.
6Short by design. The flow auto-stops when a game wins 3 in a row (or after a 5-compare cap), seeded best-match-first with the just-rejected hero pick excluded. The progress ticks under the header show how short it is.
7The chip = your chosen intent. Carried from the intent screen ("Fast & fun"), it scopes the pool and is tappable to switch — it replaces a raw duration filter. A too-narrow intent routes to the cold-start screen (Frame 4).
8Intent leads the flow. Frame 1 asks "what are you in the mood for?" and scopes the pool by genre + time-commitment (fast loop vs. big-game progress), so cards face off on like terms. Orange tags = each option's internal filter (wireframe-only).
