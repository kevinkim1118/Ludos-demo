Wireframe — Onboarding & Personality Quiz — Mobile (412px · flow board)
The mobile first-run flow. Same spine as desktop: quiz-first, scenario/vibe questions, named archetype + four taste axes, skippable. The quiz seeds the taste rung of the trust ladder before any play history exists — the cold-start fix for "Your type," paired with Steam-connect for the friends rung. Each option still loads taste axes (tagged orange for the wireframe only). Reuses the locked phone frame, tokens, hero border, trust-tier, and game-card grammar. Example throughout: Kevin → The Completionist.
Annotations — how onboarding does its job
1Quiz is the cold-start taste lever — fills "Your type" the way Steam fills friends, so day-one users aren't stranded on the global tier. It leads onboarding.
2Scenario / vibe questions — no game-recognition needed; works for lapsed/casual players. Each option loads taste axes (orange tags are wireframe-only).
3Explicit Back / Next at the bottom of every question; the final See your result stays greyed until all six are answered. Progress bar up top, Exit quiz always in the corner. ~6 questions, ~90s.
4Two-level result — a shareable named archetype is the hero; the four axes sit beneath as the "here's why" and the engine layer. Result scrolls on mobile.
5Immediate payoff — "players like you" is the now-warm taste rail, previewed (horizontal carousel) right on the result.
6Steam is the matched lever — ladder shown vertically: taste warm from quiz, friends still cold → connect. Both skippable.
7Skip is always there, never free — copy makes the global-tier fallback legible instead of hiding it.
8Lands on Discover with both personal rungs warm — the bottom-tab home surface, populated rather than collapsed to cold-start.
