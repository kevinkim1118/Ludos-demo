Wireframe — Onboarding & Personality Quiz — Desktop (flow board)
The first-run flow, and the last of the four core flows to reach wireframe. The quiz is load-bearing, not novelty: it seeds the taste rung of the trust ladder before any play history exists — the cold-start fix for "people who share your profile," exactly as Steam-connect is the cold-start fix for the friends rung. Mechanic is scenario / vibe choices (no game-recognition required); the result is a named archetype backed by four taste axes. Quiz-first, skippable. Grayscale lo-fi; reuses the locked tokens, hero border, button, trust-tier, and game-card grammar. Same example throughout: Kevin → The Completionist.
Annotations — how onboarding does its job
1The quiz is the cold-start taste lever. It fills the "Your type" rung the same way Steam-connect fills the friends rung — so a day-one user isn't stranded on the global (least-trusted) tier. This is why it leads onboarding.
2Scenario / vibe questions, not game pairings. No game-recognition required, so it works for lapsed and casual players. Each option quietly loads one or more taste axes (tagged here in orange — not shown to the real user).
3Explicit Back / Next at the bottom of every question; the final See your result stays greyed until all six are answered. Progress bar up top, Exit quiz always in the corner. ~6 questions, ~90 seconds.
4Two-level result. A shareable named archetype is the hero (the identity payoff); the four taste axes sit beneath it as the "here's why" evidence — and as the engine layer that powers taste-match.
5The result pays off immediately — "players like you really liked these" is the now-warm taste rail, previewed right on the result screen. The quiz's value becomes visible before the user even reaches the app.
6Steam-connect is the matched second lever. Framed against the same trust ladder: taste rung now warm (from the quiz), friends rung still cold → connect to fill it. Both skippable; skipping is honest about the cost.
7Skip is always available but never free. Skipping the quiz drops the user to the global tier until they've rated enough games; the copy makes that trade legible rather than hiding it.
8Lands on Discover with both personal rungs warm — the existing home surface, now populated rather than collapsed to cold-start. Onboarding's whole job is to hand off a non-empty trust ladder.
