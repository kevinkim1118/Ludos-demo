Annotations — the post-finish moment (Option B)
1Non-blocking nudge, not a modal. A dismissible bar points to the rating prompt below. Nothing blocks the page; skipping it is one tap and leaves no dead-end.
2Contribution hero in prompt mode. Same module that holds your review once submitted — here it's empty: the four-choice scale unselected, an optional review field, a submit that activates once you pick a rating.
3Status shows Finished; playthrough line carries over from the finished page.
4The pull. The comparison module's "you" anchor is empty and points back up to the prompt — the module literally can't resolve until you rate. Incompleteness as gentle motivation, reinforcing the nudge without being a second competing entry point (the rating still happens in one place: the prompt above).
5The friend / taste / global tiers are fully populated — they don't depend on your rating, so the comparison is "ready and waiting" for your number to slot in.
