One page, reweighted by two conditions: tracking status (finished) and whether a review exists (yes). Not a separate "state" — this is the status=finished / review=exists cell. Now showing the four-choice sentiment rating (no numeric score) that all rating-bearing modules share.
Annotations — finished/reviewed + the new rating system
1Identity unchanged; a "Finished" marker in the hero makes your state with the game legible at a glance.
2Contribution is the hero. Your review sits at the top of the main column. The rating is the four-choice sentiment scale — really disliked / didn't like / liked / really liked — with your pick filled. No 1–10, no stars. The just-finished prompt variant is the same module with nothing selected yet.
3Tracking is one consistent three-option selector — Want / Playing / Finished — stacked, with your current status filled. Same control in every state; only which row is filled changes.
4Purchase confidence demotes to a quiet ownership + playtime line — buy decision's over. (Still the open fork: present-but-quiet vs. stripped to a pill.)
5The verdict stays "The verdict" across every state — it never becomes a different module. Once you've rated, it simply gains a "Your rating" anchor (the dark "You" row) and the tiers report how each relates to your take. Same title, same module, now with your perspective folded in.
6One rating grammar everywhere. Because individual ratings are now sentiment, every tier speaks the same language: friends and taste-matched show the same distribution visual that global always did. The divergence beat reads as validation — your type backed your read, the wider crowd was more split.
7Friend signal swaps numeric scores for sentiment pills, tagged by how each friend's take relates to yours ("same as you," "a step below").
8Discovery becomes the exit — "what to play next."
