About ELDEN RING
Elden Ring is an action-RPG, open-world masterpiece that blends FromSoftware's signature punishing combat with environmental exploration. The game replaces rigid structural guidance with organic discovery, rewarding players for exploring every dark corner of its vast, ruined world. Elden Ring stands as an evolution of the action-RPG genre that satisfies both veteran players and resilient newcomers alike.
